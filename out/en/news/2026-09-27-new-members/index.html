Following the Light, Learning Together | Welcome to Our 2026 Master's Cohort

First-year master's students·September 27, 2026

September opens a new chapter of learning and discovery. This autumn, Zerui Yi, Tianyi Wang, Jiacheng Ge, Peimiao Yu, and Wenrui Wang begin their master's studies in the Guixiang Li Laboratory at Southeast University. They bring different hometowns, university experiences, and interests to this shared journey. Let us meet our five first-year master's students and learn about the ideas and interests that inspire them.

01

New member

Zerui Yi

Education
Undergraduate studies, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Southeast University (September 2022–July 2026)
Hometown
Ganzhou, Jiangxi

Zerui Yi describes a simple outlook: a love of life. Badminton and gaming are among favorite ways to relax. Continuing from undergraduate to master's studies on a familiar campus brings new questions and possibilities. Zerui's motto encourages courage through acknowledging one's shortcomings and progress through recognizing what remains to be learned, a fitting companion for the journey ahead.

Research direction

Stability of perovskite solar cells

Motto

Find courage in acknowledging your shortcomings, and motivation in recognizing what you still need to learn.
